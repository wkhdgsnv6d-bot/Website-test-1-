Who we are

This website is operated by Acacia Kitchens, 34 Rendle Street, Aitkenvale QLD 4814, Australia. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” means Acacia Kitchens.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What information we collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us directly, including when you submit a quote request or contact form. This may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • The address of the property your enquiry relates to
  • Details about your project, including type, budget range and preferred timing
  • Photographs and plans you choose to upload
  • Any other information you include in your message

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type and the pages you visit, where website analytics are in use.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation
  • Arrange consultations, measurements and installation
  • Communicate with you about your project
  • Keep records of work we have carried out
  • Improve our services and this website

We will not use your information for unrelated marketing without your consent, and we do not sell your personal information.

Consent

Our enquiry forms include a consent checkbox. By ticking it, you consent to being contacted by Acacia Kitchens regarding your enquiry. You can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us.

Disclosure to third parties

We may share your information with third parties only where it is necessary to deliver your project or operate this website — for example with suppliers, stonemasons or installers engaged on your job, or with service providers that host this website or process form submissions on our behalf. We require those parties to handle your information appropriately.

We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information sent to us online.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Doing so will not stop you using this website.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate or out of date. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Complaints

If you believe we have breached your privacy, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be published on this page.
