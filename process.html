Our Process

Seven steps from first call to final walkthrough

You always know what happens next, who is doing it and when. This is exactly how an Acacia Kitchens project runs.

Home/Our Process
  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Call, email or send the quote form with as much or as little detail as you have. Tell us the room, roughly when you would like it done and what you are hoping to achieve. You do not need drawings or measurements at this stage.

    • No obligation and no cost
    • We respond to every enquiry
    • Plans and photos welcome but not required
  2. 02

    Consultation

    We talk through how you use the space, the look you are after, appliance choices and the budget you are working to. This is where we flag anything that will affect cost or timing, so there are no surprises later.

    • Discuss layout, storage and finishes
    • Honest guidance on budget
    • Advice on what works in this climate
  3. 03

    Measure & Design

    We measure on site and prepare a design showing cabinetry layout, finishes and hardware, so you can picture the finished room before committing to anything.

    • Accurate on-site measurement
    • Layout and finish selections
    • Revisions until the design is right
  4. 04

    Quotation

    You receive a clear, itemised quotation setting out exactly what is included. Nothing is ordered or cut until you are happy with both the design and the price.

    • Itemised and transparent
    • Inclusions clearly listed
    • Time to consider it properly
  5. 05

    Manufacture

    Your cabinetry is manufactured in our Aitkenvale workshop. Controlling manufacture in-house means we control the quality and the schedule, and can solve problems immediately.

    • Made locally in Townsville
    • Quality checked before it leaves
    • Schedule confirmed with you
  6. 06

    Installation

    Our installers fit everything on site, scribing to walls, levelling cabinetry and coordinating with your other trades. The site is left tidy at the end of every day.

    • Installed by our own team
    • Coordinated with stone, plumbing and electrical
    • Tidy, respectful of your home
  7. 07

    Final Walkthrough

    We walk the finished job with you, adjust doors and drawers, run through care and cleaning, and make sure you are genuinely happy before we call it complete.

    • Doors and drawers adjusted
    • Care and cleaning explained
    • Any final items resolved
