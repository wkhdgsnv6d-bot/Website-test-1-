2023 Winner

HIA Townsville & Central Queensland Outdoor Kitchen Award

Acacia Kitchens won the 2023 HIA Townsville & Central Queensland Outdoor Kitchen Award — judged by the Housing Industry Association against other cabinet makers and builders across the region.

Outdoor kitchens are a genuine test of cabinet making in North Queensland. Heat, humidity, salt air and rain are unforgiving on materials and hardware, and the detailing has to be right for the finish to last. Winning in this category reflects the way we specify and build for the conditions here.

Acacia Kitchens has also received HIA finalist recognition. Details of those entries will be listed here once confirmed by the business.
