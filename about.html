Our Story

A local business, built one kitchen at a time

Acacia Kitchens is a locally owned cabinet-making business with more than 20 years of experience designing, manufacturing and installing premium kitchens, cabinetry and custom joinery throughout Townsville and North Queensland.

We have built our reputation on three things: quality workmanship, attention to detail and genuinely personal service. Those are easy words to put on a website, so we would rather you judged them by the work — the way a drawer closes, the way a panel meets a wall, the way the job site looks at the end of the day.

Every project is custom designed to suit the client’s lifestyle, budget and space. Nothing is pulled off a shelf, and nothing is quoted before we understand what you are actually trying to achieve.

We work with homeowners, builders, renovators, developers and commercial clients across the region, creating practical, functional and beautiful cabinetry built to last in the North Queensland climate.

What Guides Us

How we work, and why it matters

01

Quality craftsmanship

Twenty years of cabinet making means we know which shortcuts cost you later. We do not take them. Squared carcasses, consistent gaps and properly scribed panels are the baseline, not an upgrade.

02

Premium materials

We specify quality board, hinges, runners and benchtop products chosen to handle heat, humidity and daily use. In this climate, material choice is not a detail — it is the difference between ten years and twenty.

03

Personal service

You deal directly with the people designing and building your cabinetry. Questions get answered by someone who has seen your job, not by a script.

04

Custom manufacturing

Your cabinetry is manufactured in our own Aitkenvale workshop. Controlling manufacture means we control the quality, the timeline and the ability to solve a problem on the spot.

05

Professional installation

Our own installers finish what the workshop starts, coordinating with your other trades and leaving the site tidy at the end of every day.

06

Family values

We are a local business in a city where reputation travels fast. We treat your home the way we would want ours treated, and we stand behind the work we deliver.
