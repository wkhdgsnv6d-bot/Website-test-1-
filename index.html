About Acacia Kitchens

A Townsville cabinet maker built on craft, not shortcuts

Acacia Kitchens is a locally owned cabinet-making business with more than 20 years of experience designing, manufacturing and installing premium kitchens, cabinetry and custom joinery throughout Townsville and North Queensland.

We have built our reputation on quality workmanship, attention to detail and genuinely personal service. Every project is custom designed around the way you live, the space you have and the budget you are working to — never pulled from a catalogue.

From single-room renovations to full new builds and commercial fit-outs, we work with homeowners, builders, renovators, developers and commercial clients to create cabinetry that is practical, beautiful and built to last in the North Queensland climate.

Our Process

Seven clear steps, from first call to final walkthrough

You always know what happens next, who is doing it and when.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Call, email or send the quote form. Tell us the room, the timeframe and roughly what you have in mind — no drawings needed at this stage.

  2. 02

    Consultation

    We talk through how you use the space, the look you are after, appliance choices and the budget you are working to.

  3. 03

    Measure & Design

    We measure on site and prepare a design with cabinetry layout, finishes and hardware so you can picture the finished room.

  4. 04

    Quotation

    A clear, itemised quotation. You can see exactly what is included before anything is ordered or cut.

  5. 05

    Manufacture

    Your cabinetry is manufactured in our Aitkenvale workshop, where we control the quality and the timeline.

  6. 06

    Installation

    Our installers fit everything on site, coordinate with your other trades and keep the space tidy as they go.

  7. 07

    Final Walkthrough

    We walk the finished job with you, adjust doors and drawers, and make sure you are genuinely happy before we call it done.
