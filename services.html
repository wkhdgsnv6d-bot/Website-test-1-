01 — Service

Custom Kitchens

The kitchen is where a home actually happens, so we design ours around how you cook, store and gather — not around a standard cabinet run.

Every custom kitchen starts with a measure and a conversation about bench space, appliance choices, traffic flow and where everything needs to live. We then manufacture your cabinetry in our Aitkenvale workshop and install it ourselves, so the quality is controlled from the first cut to the final adjustment.

  • Designed around your appliances, storage and bench heights
  • Soft-close drawers and quality hardware as standard
  • Full choice of door finishes, benchtops and handles
  • Manufactured locally and installed by our own team

Kitchen Renovations

Renovation work is as much about planning as it is about cabinetry. We stage the job so your kitchen is out of action for the shortest time possible, coordinate with plumbers, electricians and stonemasons, and work carefully around the parts of your home that are staying.

  • Staged to keep disruption and downtime to a minimum
  • Coordination with your plumber, electrician and stonemason
  • Careful removal and disposal of the old kitchen
  • Options to suit a range of renovation budgets
